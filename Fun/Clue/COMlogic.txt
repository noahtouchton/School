How the COM players should think:

If you have already shown a player a card you should show them that card again if possible
Dont guess cards you know other players have
Guess cards that are less likely to have based on when they show cards

Guess your own cards to learn more about what others have

Its ok to guess cards you know other people have if you are trying to disprove that people between you and them have that card


Give each suspect, weapon, and room a weight based on how likely it is that they are the secret card

Things that make a card less likely to be the secret card:
- You know a player has shown this card (the further away the player to your left the more useful this can be)
- A player has shown a card when this card was asked for but you are not sure if it was this one
- The card is in your hand (this can be very useful)

Things that make a card more likely to be the secret card:
- Several players have said no to this card. 

Use for other players cards
Card Score = (c1*Number of Players who said no to this card + baseline)*(Distance between owner of card and you / total number of players || 1 if you dont know who has it)
